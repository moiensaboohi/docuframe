# cinematic-dark — Cinematic dark

**What it is:** Premium feature-documentary look: near-black warm gradient, full-frame photographs, slow drifting camera, thin gold serif titles, cinema bars.

**Use for:** prestige documentary, biography, brand and founder stories.
**Avoid for:** Fast list videos and kids' content.

**Recipe:** panel = glass, photo = full, title = serif, number = counter, motion = slide, accents = none, source = plain, lower_third = line, letterbox = True, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
