# tech-gradient — Night-gradient tech

**What it is:** Deep violet gradient, glowing glass cards, mint data rings and tight geometric type. Premium, modern, made for tech and money.

**Use for:** technology and AI, finance and markets, startups and products.
**Avoid for:** History before photography; rural and nature stories.

**Recipe:** panel = glass, photo = glass, title = kinetic, number = ring, motion = pop, accents = shapes, source = chip, lower_third = chip, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
