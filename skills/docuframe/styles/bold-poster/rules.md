# bold-poster — Bold poster

**What it is:** Neo-brutalist poster energy: saturated yellow ground, black ink outlines with hard offset shadows, giant black type, red hits.

**Use for:** hot takes and rankings, internet culture, sport and music.
**Avoid for:** Grief, medicine, anything delicate.

**Recipe:** panel = outline, photo = outline, title = kinetic, number = counter, motion = slap, accents = shapes, source = chip, lower_third = bar, shadow = 1.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
