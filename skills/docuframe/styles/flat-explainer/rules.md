# flat-explainer — Clean flat explainer

**What it is:** Bright off-white ground with a dot grid, rounded cards with soft shadows, bold geometric type, springy pop-ins and progress rings.

**Use for:** science and how-it-works, business and finance basics, education for all ages.
**Avoid for:** True crime and grief: it is cheerful by design.

**Recipe:** panel = card, photo = frame, title = kinetic, number = ring, motion = pop, accents = none, source = chip, lower_third = chip, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
