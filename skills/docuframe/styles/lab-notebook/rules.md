# lab-notebook — Science lab notebook

**What it is:** Graph-paper ground, sticky notes, taped-in photos, handwritten headings and blue-ink circles: a scientist's notebook come alive.

**Use for:** science and medicine, inventions, how experiments worked.
**Avoid for:** Hard news and conflict.

**Recipe:** panel = sticky, photo = tape, title = kinetic, number = counter, motion = pop, accents = shapes, source = chip, lower_third = chip, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
