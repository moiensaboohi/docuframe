# editorial-collage — Editorial paper collage

**What it is:** Torn paper, duotone cut-out photos, bold condensed type slapped onto a warm paper ground; scraps of colour around every idea.

**Use for:** culture and history explainers, politics and economics, people and ideas.
**Avoid for:** Tragedies with victims (the playful scraps feel wrong) and data-heavy charts.

**Recipe:** panel = torn, photo = cutout, title = torn, number = counter, motion = slap, accents = scraps, source = chip, lower_third = bar, shadow = 0.9.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
