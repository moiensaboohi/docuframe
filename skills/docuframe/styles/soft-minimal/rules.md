# soft-minimal — Soft editorial minimal

**What it is:** Calm magazine minimalism: warm paper-white ground, elegant serif headlines, quiet sans text, terracotta details and generous white space.

**Use for:** culture, food and travel, biography and people, slow, thoughtful essays.
**Avoid for:** Fast news and hype.

**Recipe:** motion = rise, source = quiet, lower_third = line, accents = none, panel = none, photo = rounded, title = kinetic, number = counter, layout = editorial, align = start, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
