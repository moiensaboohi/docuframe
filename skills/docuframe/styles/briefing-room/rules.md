# briefing-room — Dark briefing room

**What it is:** Deep navy situation-room look: glowing grid, glass panels with cyan edges, viewfinder brackets, monospace data and amber numbers.

**Use for:** geopolitics and conflict, intelligence and investigations, cyber and space.
**Avoid for:** Light, human-interest stories.

**Recipe:** panel = glass, photo = glass, title = kinetic, number = counter, motion = wipe, accents = corners, source = chip, lower_third = chip, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
