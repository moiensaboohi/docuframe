# minimal-mono — Minimal monochrome

**What it is:** Near-black ground, white type, nothing else: large kinetic headlines, hairline frames and slow wipes. Lets the words carry it.

**Use for:** essays and opinion, technology, philosophy and big ideas.
**Avoid for:** Kids' content; stories that need many photos in colour.

**Recipe:** panel = glass, photo = frame, title = kinetic, number = counter, motion = wipe, accents = none, source = plain, lower_third = line, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
