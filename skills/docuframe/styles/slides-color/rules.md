# slides-color — Colour-block slides

**What it is:** Bold presentation slides: title and chapter slides on a full indigo colour field with white type, content slides on white with soft cards and indigo accents.

**Use for:** courses and workshops, company and team updates, pitch-style explainers.
**Avoid for:** Slow, atmospheric documentary.

**Recipe:** motion = rise, source = quiet, lower_third = line, accents = none, panel = surface, photo = rounded, title = kinetic, number = counter, layout = deck, align = start, flat = True, title_fill = True, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
