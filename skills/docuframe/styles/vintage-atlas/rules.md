# vintage-atlas — Vintage atlas

**What it is:** Old printed-map feel: parchment ground with a faint graticule, sepia photos taped in, elegant serif headings, oxblood ink.

**Use for:** exploration and geography, empires and trade routes, historical journeys.
**Avoid for:** Modern technology and fast news.

**Recipe:** panel = paper, photo = tape, title = serif, number = counter, motion = drop, accents = none, source = plain, lower_third = line, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
