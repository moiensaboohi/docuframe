# studio-dark — Studio dark (keynote stage)

**What it is:** A black keynote stage: centred white headlines, big numbers in a blue-to-violet gradient, quiet grey supporting lines and photos that glow softly out of the dark.

**Use for:** technology launches and AI, space and science, big-number stories.
**Avoid for:** Warm human stories that need a light touch.

**Recipe:** motion = rise, source = quiet, lower_third = line, accents = none, panel = none, photo = rounded, title = kinetic, number = counter, layout = editorial, align = center, eyebrow_sub = True, number_gradient = ['grad1', 'grad2'], shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
