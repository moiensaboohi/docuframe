# evidence-board — Cinematic evidence board

**What it is:** one continuous camera glides across a dark cork wall. Photos are pinned with paper
borders, joined by red string; stamps, name bars and pen marks land on the spoken word; big numbers
are distressed yellow type. Warm grade, grain, vignette, a spotlight on the current cluster.

**Use for:** true stories, history, crime and investigations, biographies, "how did this happen".
**Avoid for:** cheerful product stories, kids' science (too dark), data-heavy explainers with many charts.

**Judge a still by:**
- The current cluster is lit, the rest dims; one hero per beat.
- Photos are black-and-white or sepia graded (consistent); colour only for documents/covers.
- Red is for marks, labels and string only; yellow only for numbers and highlights.
- Titles sit on the red bar, auto-fitted; nothing touches the frame edge.
- String connects consecutive photos and never crosses a face.
