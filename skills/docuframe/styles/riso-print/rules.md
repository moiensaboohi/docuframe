# riso-print — Risograph print

**What it is:** Two-ink riso zine: blue-and-pink duotone photos on cream stock, halftone dots, slightly off-register energy, condensed blue type.

**Use for:** art, music and design history, subculture stories, city and place portraits.
**Avoid for:** Corporate and finance topics.

**Recipe:** panel = paper, photo = cutout, title = kinetic, number = counter, motion = slap, accents = halftone, source = chip, lower_third = bar, shadow = 0.85.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
