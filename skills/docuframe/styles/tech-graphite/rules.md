# tech-graphite — Graphite tech

**What it is:** Engineering-drawing calm: flat graphite ground with a faint grid, crisp grotesque type, monospace labels, signal-amber numbers on thin rings. No glow, no gradients.

**Use for:** technology and AI, finance and markets, engineering and products.
**Avoid for:** History before photography; rural and nature stories.

**Recipe:** panel = card, photo = frame, title = kinetic, number = ring, motion = wipe, accents = none, source = chip, lower_third = line, shadow = 0.0.

**Judge a still by:**
- One hero per beat; everything else quieter.
- Type never touches the frame edge; nothing important in the bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame.
- Colours come only from this pack's tokens; numbers always carry their source.
- Motion lands on the spoken word, then holds still long enough to read.
