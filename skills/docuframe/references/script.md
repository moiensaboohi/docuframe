# Writing the script

- **Hook (first 10 s):** state the payoff or the strangest true fact. Viewers decide in the first
  seconds whether the video matches its title; most drop-off happens in the first minute.
- **One idea per line**, 8–25 words. Each line becomes one caption cue group and usually one beat.
- `text` is what captions show ("1919", "2.3 million"); `spoken` is what the narrator says
  ("nineteen nineteen", "two point three million"). Beats' `at` words refer to `spoken`.
- Link every factual line to `facts` ids; mark rhetoric with `"opinion": true`.
- Chapters (`takes`): 3+ chapters of 10 s or more give YouTube chapters. Name them in `chapter` beats.
- Close: answer the hook, then one line that sends viewers on (a question, the next episode).
- Don't pad. A 6-minute script is ~850 words at a calm documentary pace (~140 wpm).
